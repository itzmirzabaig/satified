About Satified

Satified is a free SAT Math practice site built by one student. No company behind it, no funnel, no growth team: just a question bank built carefully because the existing options kept missing the mark.

Why it exists

My name is Mirza N. Baig, and I graduated from Charlotte Amalie High School in St. Thomas in 2026.

My class kept losing math teachers. We lost the calculus teacher. We had no Algebra 2 teacher. Our only geometry teacher was out for about three months, and in that time we did almost nothing. We still passed our classes, because passing a class and learning the math are two different things. The difference only showed up when something measured us: about 4 percent of my class was proficient in math on the Smarter Balanced assessment, and the class below us was around 1.5 percent. Every year, there was a little less to work with.

I got through it by teaching myself. That worked for me on the SAT, and it is not a solution. It only helps the one student willing to spend a year doing the school's job alone, and I watched classmates who were every bit as capable get nothing.

Then there was the second wall. The practice platforms whose questions actually felt like the test, especially at medium and hard, all had a price on them. The free ones recycled the same handful of problems until you were memorizing answers instead of learning patterns.

So I built the tool I wanted, and made it the thing I needed it to be: real digital SAT difficulty, sharpest exactly where scores are decided, unlimited because every question regenerates, and free, because a paywall would lock out the students I built it for. The longer version of that story is here.

Every question is a generator

A static question dies the second time you see it: you remember the answer and learn nothing. Every question on Satified is a generator, so each time it loads it rebuilds with fresh numbers, a fresh context, and reshuffled choices. The pattern is the thing you master, because the pattern is the only thing that survives regeneration.

That mirrors what the SAT itself does. It never repeats a question at you; it repeats patterns. Practicing on generators trains for exactly that.

Verified, question by question

The bank holds 1,483 questions across the digital SAT's 4 domains and 19 skills. Every answer, every figure, and every explanation has been independently verified, checked separately from the code that generates it. A practice question with a wrong key teaches wrong math, so this was the slowest part of building the site and the least skippable.

Free, with nothing attached

No account, no ads, no premium tier, no email capture. A student built this for students, and the whole point was removing the wall between you and honest practice. If it helps you score what you are capable of, that is the transaction.
