What Satified is

Satified is a free practice site for the Math half of the digital SAT. The bank holds 1,483 questions spread across all four domains and all nineteen tested skills, each written to the difficulty bands the real exam uses, from the easy openers of Module 1 to the problems that close the hard second module.

You can filter the whole bank by domain, by individual skill, or by difficulty, so a session can be as broad as mixed review or as narrow as hard probability only. Every question ships with a verified answer and a worked explanation, and the same Desmos calculator that appears on test day sits one click away.

Why regeneration beats a static list

Most free SAT resources are fixed lists. Work through one twice and your score starts lying to you, because you are recognizing answers instead of producing them. Satified questions are generators, and that changes what practice does to your brain.

Step 01

You answer a question

A percentages question asks about a $40 sweater marked up 15 percent. You work it, check the explanation, and move on.

Step 02

It comes back different

Load the skill again and the sweater is a $62 jacket at 20 percent, with the answer choices rebuilt and reshuffled.

Step 03

The method is what remains

You cannot memorize a moving target. What sticks is the setup, which is the only thing the real test rewards.

Questions before you start

Is Satified actually free?
Yes, completely. All 1,483 questions, the adaptive practice tests, the explanations, and the Desmos calculator cost nothing. There is no paywall, no trial, and no ad.
How many practice questions are there?
There are 1,483 distinct questions, but each one regenerates with new numbers and contexts every time it loads, so the effective supply does not run out.
Does the practice match the real digital SAT?
The questions follow the digital SAT blueprint: the same four domains, the same nineteen skills, the same difficulty tiers, and the same calculator policy, with Desmos available on every question.
Do I need to create an account?
No. There is nothing to sign up for and no email to hand over. Open the study page and the first question is seconds away.
How is this different from question banks that repeat?
A static bank teaches you its own answer key over time. Because Satified rebuilds every question with fresh values, a repeat visit is a genuinely new problem, and your accuracy reflects skill instead of memory.
