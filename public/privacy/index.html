Privacy

PRIVACY POLICY · EFFECTIVE JULY 9, 2026

Satified is a free SAT Math practice site operated by an individual. It has no accounts, no ads, and no interest in who you are. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain English, what happens with information when you use satified.org. It is linked in the footer of every page so you can read it at any time.

1. What We Collect

Essentially nothing personal. Satified has no accounts, no login, and no forms that ask for your name, email address, or anything else about you. We do not collect, store, or sell personal information.

The site saves a few preferences, such as your theme choice, in your browser’s localStorage. That information stays on your device. It is never sent to us, and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data for satified.org, and the site will simply start fresh. Separately, the site sets a first party analytics cookie to measure overall usage, which is explained in the Analytics section below.

Like nearly every website, Satified relies on a hosting provider to deliver its pages. Our host, Cloudflare, may process standard server logs, which can include IP addresses and basic request details, in order to serve the site and protect it from attacks. We do not use these logs to identify visitors.

2. What We Do Not Do

Because we collect no personal information, most privacy risks simply do not exist here. To be explicit:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade any data about you.
  • We do not show ads or work with advertising networks.
  • We do not track you across other websites.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or try to identify you personally.

3. Children’s Privacy

Most Satified visitors are students between 13 and 18 years old, and some may be younger. The site collects no personal information from anyone, and that includes children under 13. Because nothing personal is collected from any visitor, the site is designed to align with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

If you believe a child has somehow sent personal information to us, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

4. Third Party Services

Cloudflare Pages hosts and delivers this site. Cloudflare’s handling of network data is described in its own privacy policy.

The study app loads a small number of libraries, such as MathJax for displaying math and the Desmos calculator, from content delivery networks. When your browser fetches those files, the service that hosts them receives a standard web request from your browser, which can include your IP address. That is how loading any file on the internet works. These libraries are used only to display math and provide the calculator, and Satified sends them no information about you.

5. Analytics

Satified uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used, for example how many people open a page or use the AI tutor, and to tell new visitors from returning ones. To do this it sets a first party analytics cookie in your browser that holds a random identifier. That identifier is not your name and is not linked to who you are.

Google Signals and advertising features are turned off, so this is plain first party analytics: it is not used to show you ads, it does not follow you to other websites, and the data is never sold. Reports show aggregate patterns, such as which pages and tutor features get used, rather than a dossier on any one person.

The AI tutor also records anonymous signals, such as whether an explanation was marked helpful or unhelpful, so the tutoring can be improved over time.

If you would rather not be counted, most browsers let you block cookies or analytics scripts, or you can use private browsing, and the site works exactly the same either way.

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The current version will always be posted at this address, with its effective date shown at the top. Continuing to use the site after an update means the new version applies.
