## Gallery (what to show, in order)

Product Hunt shows a thumbnail plus a gallery. Images look best at 1270 x 760. Aim for 4 to 6:

1. A short GIF or the hero shot: the homepage "Crack the SAT" acorn. This is your first impression, make it the strongest.
2. The practice app: a question card with answer choices visible.
3. The AI tutor panel open, mid conversation, showing a hint. This is your differentiator, feature it prominently.
4. The full practice test or the results screen with the 200 to 800 score.
5. Optional: the score calculator, or a domain page showing the skill coverage.

A 10 to 20 second screen recording of clicking "Tutor" and getting a hint, used as the first gallery item, converts far better than a static image. If you can make one, do.

Thumbnail/logo: your gold "S" acorn mark.

## Launch day

**When:** Schedule for 12:01 AM Pacific Time, on a Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday. Product Hunt's day resets at midnight PT, so launching then gives you a full 24 hours. Avoid weekends and Mondays (lower traffic and more competition respectively). Product Hunt lets you schedule the launch in advance, so you do not have to be awake at midnight.

**The play, in order:**
1. A few days before, fully complete your maker profile: real photo, short bio, a couple of follows. A blank profile hurts credibility.
2. Schedule the launch (or submit it) with all the copy and gallery above.
3. The moment it goes live, post the first comment above.
4. Tell your people it is live and ask them to take a real look and leave honest thoughts. Send them the launch link.
5. Reply to every single comment, fast, all day. Engagement is what Product Hunt's ranking rewards.
6. Share the launch link where it is genuinely welcome: your socials, relevant Discords, school groups.

**Do not:**
- Do not run an upvote ring or ask for "just upvote me" with no engagement. Product Hunt detects and penalizes coordinated voting. Ask for genuine looks and feedback instead; the votes follow.
- Do not launch and disappear. A launch with no maker replies dies. Block out the day.
- Do not oversell. Your honest "one student, free forever" story is the strongest thing you have. Lead with it.

## Step by step to submit

1. Go to producthunt.com and sign in (or create an account). Complete your profile first.
2. Top right, click **Submit** (or "Launch a product").
3. Paste the URL https://satified.org, then the name, tagline, and description above.
4. Add the topics: Education, Artificial Intelligence, EdTech.
5. Upload the thumbnail and the gallery images or GIF.
6. Add yourself as a maker.
7. Choose **Schedule** and set it for 12:01 AM PT on a Tue, Wed, or Thu (or launch now if you prefer).
8. Save. When it goes live, immediately post the first comment and start replying.

That is the whole launch. After this, your SEO and launch checklist is complete.
