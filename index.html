Why this exists

Not a math course.
A pattern gym.

Satified exists to do one thing: drill the exact patterns and problem types the College Board uses on the SAT Math section. Every question is intentional, built to capture almost every concept the test could throw at you.

This is not a site for learning basic algebra or geometry; Khan Academy already does that well. The problem is that most platforms don't match the difficulty and structure of real SAT questions — especially at medium and hard levels. The ones that come close usually aren't free.

And there's a lack of true variety. Many sites repeat similar problems with small edits. Here, every question regenerates with different numbers and setups, so you practice the same concept until you master the pattern, not memorize the answer.
